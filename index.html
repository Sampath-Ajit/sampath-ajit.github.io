01 About

I'm a security engineer who treats the SDLC as the attack surface. At Citi's regulated banking platform I built and owned the automated SAST/DAST/SCA pipelines that cut API vulnerabilities by 90% — and sustained that reduction through continuous monitoring.

Since then I've embedded Veracode, Checkmarx, Burp Suite and Snyk into enterprise CI/CD, led STRIDE threat-modeling workshops with engineering teams, hardened Kubernetes and AWS IAM with policy-as-code, stood up Elastic SIEM detections that caught real credential-stuffing patterns, and run full NIST 800-61 incident response cycles end-to-end.

My work is documented, not asserted: Project RAMPART, my open two-volume AppSec engineering curriculum, shows every pipeline, detection rule, and fix step by step.
